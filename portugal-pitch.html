Why split — and the honest cost of it

Why not Azores-only? Two hard facts from the research: (1) São Miguel has effectively no single villa that sleeps 20 — the stock tops out around 7BR/sleeps-16, so any Azores stay means adjacent-unit clusters; and (2) even in July (statistically the driest, sunniest month) the Azores microclimate can serve mist and cloud on any given day. Betting all nine days of a once-in-a-lifetime reunion on it is a real gamble.

Why not mainland-only? Because the family asked for the Azores, and the Azores deliver something no competing pitch can: resident sperm whales 365 days a year, twin crater lakes, and lunch cooked in volcanic ground. Four days there is the sweet spot — long enough for the greatest hits, short enough that a cloudy day costs you nothing.

Is moving 20 people mid-trip worth it? It costs one travel day and roughly $5,000–$6,500 in intra-Portugal flights for the whole group. The move is unusually clean here because tickets can be booked open-jaw (fly into Ponta Delgada, fly home from Lisbon/Faro) — nobody backtracks. We say yes, it's worth it — but if the family votes for zero-move simplicity, Azores-only is the fallback and saves that $5–6.5k.

07Ground Logistics & the Toddler Test

Wheels for 20

São Miguel: 3 x 9-seat minivans (~$95–130/day each in July) — ~$1,400–$1,600 for the leg. Caldera roads are winding and can be foggy: for the two big touring days, a 22-seat minibus with driver is €550/day including fuel — worth it so every adult gets to look at the view instead of the guardrail.

Algarve: 3 x 9-seaters from Faro (~£250–400/wk each) — ~$1,500–$2,000. Flat, modern, easy roads. Car seats: rent €5–8/day or bring your own.

Kids, food, medical

Beaches: Azores = dramatic volcanic sand, limited but real (the recommended resort sits on one). Algarve = the sandy-cove jackpot.

Food: the most kid-forgiving cuisine in Europe — grilled chicken, fresh bread, pastéis de nata. English widely spoken in both regions.

Medical: full public hospital with ER in Ponta Delgada (Hospital do Divino Espírito Santo, 20–40 min from anywhere we'd stay); the Algarve has both public hospitals (Faro, Portimão) and private clinics (Hospital Particular do Algarve) minutes from the villa. Pharmacies everywhere.

09The Honest Ledger

A pitch that hides its weaknesses deserves to lose the family vote. Here is the full ledger.

Why Portugal genuinely wins

  • Whales in July, guaranteed habitat. Sperm whales are resident in the Azores 365 days a year — a fact, not a hope. (Maui's humpbacks are gone by summer.)
  • Best value in Western Europe. Dinner for twenty at ~$600, groceries ~35% below US prices — the budget stretches further than Greece, Norway, or Alaska by a wide margin.
  • The Boston miracle: a ~5-hour nonstop to Europe. And open-jaw ticketing means the mid-trip move wastes zero backtracking.
  • Two vacations in one: volcanic wonder + guaranteed beach week. If Azores weather sulks for a day, the trip's second act is already booked in the sun.
  • True 20-under-one-roof exists on the Algarve leg (10–11BR villas with real, published prices).
  • Genuinely multigenerational: whale catamarans, thermal pools, drive-up crater views — the 67-year-old and the 3.5-year-old do the headline days together.
  • Safe, easy, English-friendly — consistently ranked among the world's most peaceful countries.

What we're honestly weighing

  • The mid-trip move is real: one full travel day herding 20 people + ~$5,000–$6,500 in extra flights. It's the price of having both Portugals.
  • Azores weather roulette: even in its best month, mist and cloud can crash any given day. We've capped exposure at 4 nights, but the whale trip can be rescheduled by swell.
  • No single 20-sleeper villa on São Miguel — the family sleeps in adjacent villas there, not one house. (One pool, one lawn — but not one roof.)
  • Algarve July = peak heat and peak crowds. 90°F afternoons, full beaches, busy restaurants. Manageable with a villa pool and early starts — but it is high season, priced and packed accordingly.
  • The sea is Atlantic-cool everywhere (68–72°F). Kids won't care; anyone expecting Caribbean bathwater will.
  • Long hauls from the West: SLC and SoCal families face 12–16-hour door-to-door journeys with a connection — longer than Maui, comparable to Greece.
  • Toddler skips a few things (kayaks, Ferraria hot spring); boat tours and thermal pools are the workarounds.
  • Base-case lodging runs ~$30–34k — at or slightly above target unless the value pairing is chosen.

Why Portugal Takes the Vote

Every pitch on the table has one great trick. Greece has the villa. Alaska has the glaciers. Maui has the easy flight west. Norway has the fjords. Slovenia has the lake.

Portugal has a whale-rich volcanic archipelago AND a warm golden coast in the same country, at the lowest cost-of-living in Western Europe — with a 5-hour nonstop for Boston, published prices on real 20-person lodging, a thermal pool the toddler and the grandparents will share, and food bills that make the accountant in every family smile.

We didn't hide the cons: a moving day, a cloud risk, a hot week in high season. We planned around each one. That's not a fantasy pitch — that's a trip that will actually work for all twenty of us.
